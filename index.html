Six things the data says

One offer static carries the account

"Shop Bundle Deals – Up To 50% OFF" (two placements) has 5,494 likes, 4,944 comments and 805 shares. It started 3 Nov 2025 and was still running on 28 Sep 2026. Nothing else is close.

The problem is named in the copy

The best ad's description is "Stop Indoor Peeing And Anxiety In Dogs With This Science-Driven Solution". The other top dog ad leads with "anxious dog feel safe and calm". Name one pain, then show the fix.

Identity beats product for cats

"There are 3 types of cat ladies. Which one are you" runs in four image variants with the same words. Likes range from 146 to 1,649. The image is what changes the result, so this is the best template to iterate.

Long-running means it pays

The offer ad and the cat-ladies set are both still live after 216 to 329 days. An ad that runs that long is being funded because it converts. Model new statics on these first.

Story hooks get comments, not always likes

"We stopped charging for shipping. One email is why." drew 300 comments on 343 likes in Australia. Good for reach and curiosity, weaker as a pure conversion image.

Brazil is small and separate

The Portuguese page has 4 ads and 114 likes in total. Treat it as a localisation test, not a source of winning patterns.

Production notes

Specs and checklist

Sizes

Feed, primary
1080 × 1350 px (4:5). Every ad in this set is a single image or a carousel card, so start here.
Feed, square
1080 × 1080 px (1:1) as a second crop.
Stories / Reels
1080 × 1920 px (9:16). Keep text out of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px.
Carousel card
1080 × 1080 px. GiroCat uses 10 cards.
CTA button
Set in Ads Manager, not in the image. Winners use "Shop now" (11 of 15), "Learn more" and "See details".

Text on the image

  • One headline, seven words or fewer where possible.
  • One number or offer badge (50% OFF, 3 diffusers, 4.8/5).
  • Product visible without needing to read the text.
  • Brand mark and therapetmd.com placed away from the platform UI edges.
  • Check legibility at phone width before export.
  • Pick one angle from the playbook per static. Do not mix two.
  • Make at least 3 image variants per winning headline. The cat-ladies set shows an 11× spread from image alone.
  • Make separate dog and cat versions. Dog ads lead with peeing and anxiety, cat ads with identity.
  • Keep offer, price and shipping claims identical to the landing page.
  • Localise text for Brazil (PT-BR) rather than translating layouts one to one.
  • Name files angle_headline_variant_size so results map back to the brief.
