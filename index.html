TheraPet winning statics

The 11 best-liked TheraPet static ads (US, UK, UAE, CA, AU, NZ, NL), what they have in common, and what to make next.

What wins

  1. Real photos, no text on the image. Five of the six ads above 800 likes are plain phone-style photos. The headline and offer go in the ad copy.
  2. Show the product large. Best cat photo: duck diffuser and orange box filling the frame (1,649 likes). Worst: cat portrait with a small box (146 likes). Same headline.
  3. Show quantity for offers. The top ad (5,211 likes) is a photo of several diffusers and refills lined up on a counter.
  4. Use current packaging. Green box for dogs, orange for cats, duck diffuser. Blue "TheraPetMD" boxes are only in the Nov 2025 ads.
