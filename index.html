Six things the data says

One offer static carries the account

"Shop Bundle Deals – Up To 50% OFF" (two placements) has 5,494 likes, 4,944 comments and 805 shares. It started 3 Nov 2025 and was still running on 28 Sep 2026. Nothing else is close.

The problem is named in the copy

The best ad's description is "Stop Indoor Peeing And Anxiety In Dogs With This Science-Driven Solution". The other top dog ad leads with "anxious dog feel safe and calm". Name one pain, then show the fix.

Real photos beat designed graphics

Five of the six ads above 800 likes are plain photos with no text on the image: product on a counter, a smiling owner, a cat sniffing the diffuser. The one designed graphic (price, badges, puppy) has the third-highest likes.

Same headline, 11× spread

"There are 3 types of cat ladies. Which one are you" runs over four different photos. Likes range from 146 to 1,649, so the photo decides the result. The set has run 216 days and is still live, which makes it the best template to iterate.

Two looks in the packaging

The two November 2025 ads show blue "TheraPetMD" boxes. The 2026 ads show the current green (dog) and orange (cat) TheraPet boxes with the duck diffuser. Use the current packaging in new statics.

Brazil sells a different product

The Portuguese page advertises a cat scratcher and a steam brush with bold text on the image. It has 4 ads and 114 likes in total, so treat it as a test, not a source of winning patterns.

Production notes

Specs and checklist

Sizes

Feed, primary
1080 × 1350 px (4:5). Every ad in this set is a single image or a carousel card, so start here.
Feed, square
1080 × 1080 px (1:1) as a second crop.
Stories / Reels
1080 × 1920 px (9:16). Keep text out of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px.
Carousel card
1080 × 1080 px. GiroCat uses 10 cards.
CTA button
Set in Ads Manager, not in the image. Winners use "Shop now" (11 of 15), "Learn more" and "See details".

Text on the image

  • US winners carry no text on the image. The headline and offer sit in the ad copy.
  • If you do use text (the promo graphic), keep to a price, a sale line and three short badges.
  • Show the current packaging: green for dogs, orange for cats, with the duck diffuser.
  • Shoot on a real surface in daylight. Avoid studio backdrops.
  • Check legibility at phone width before export.
  • Pick one angle from the playbook per static. Do not mix two.
  • Make at least 3 photo variants per winning headline. The cat-ladies set shows an 11× spread from the photo alone.
  • Make separate dog and cat versions. Dog ads lead with peeing and anxiety, cat ads with identity.
  • Keep offer, price and shipping claims identical to the landing page.
  • Localise text for Brazil (PT-BR) rather than translating layouts one to one.
  • Name files angle_headline_variant_size so results map back to the brief.
